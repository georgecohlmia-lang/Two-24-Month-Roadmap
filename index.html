The Three Critical Assumptions

Name them before the math buries them

Every projection rests on two or three core assumptions. Everything else is math. Name them explicitly. If any one breaks, the model breaks — so you need to know which numbers you're most exposed on.

01 New Customers per Month by M6

The number. Then ask honestly: can your pipeline and network produce this?

02 Average Revenue per Customer

Are you modeling the high end of what the market will pay? If so, your model is fragile.

03 Sales Cycle Length

The assumption that bites hardest. If you assume two weeks and actual is eight, your cash timing is off by months.
